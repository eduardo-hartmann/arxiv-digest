## Who I am
Astrophysicist (PhD student) working on galaxy spectroscopy, stellar population
analysis and machine learning applied to large spectroscopic surveys. My work
bridges physical modelling (stellar population synthesis) and data-driven
methods in extragalactic astrophysics.

## Core topics (rank these highest)
- Stellar population synthesis models; full-spectrum fitting; star-formation
  and chemical-enrichment histories of galaxies from spectra
- Galaxy spectroscopy from large surveys: DESI, SDSS/MaNGA, 4MOST, WEAVE,
  PFS, JWST/NIRSpec
- Machine learning on astronomical spectra: representation learning,
  contrastive / CLIP-style multimodal models (spectra ↔ images ↔ properties),
  foundation models for astronomy, emulators, simulation-based inference
- Inferring galaxy physical properties (stellar mass, age, metallicity, SFR,
  dust) from spectra and photometry, and the uncertainties of those inferences

## Secondary topics (interesting, rank medium)
- Galaxy evolution and quenching, especially results driven by spectroscopy
- Methods papers in ML that could transfer to scientific data: self-supervised
  learning, contrastive learning, multimodal alignment, uncertainty
  quantification, anomaly detection, normalizing flows / diffusion for inference
- Large-survey data releases and pipelines relevant to spectroscopy

## Less interested (rank low unless it is a big deal for the field)
- Pure LLM / agent / benchmark papers with no link to scientific data
- Reinforcement learning, robotics, recommender systems
- Detailed single-object studies unrelated to stellar populations
